## MR-RATE statistics

The charts show aggregate counts computed from the MR-RATE metadata, report and label tables
(batch 00 and the label table). No images, report text or patient-level rows are included, and
cells with fewer than 5 studies are withheld. MR-RATE is released by Forithmus under
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 with additional access terms. See https://huggingface.co/datasets/Forithmus/MR-RATE
